# AI Products

Three AI products — from idea to shipped interface.

Each one is documented separately: what it does, what makes it hold up, and what it actually looks like in practice. Pick one below, or open its folder for the full write-up (English `README.md`, Russian `README_RU.md`).

## [Forge](./Forge) — describe your product, get a full site in 60 seconds

![Forge](Forge/screenshots/forge-landing-hero.png)

An AI site generator that turns a one-sentence product description into a real, deployable React + Vite site — picking the design, writing the copy, and assembling every section on its own. No template picker, no drag-and-drop editor.

→ [Read more](./Forge/README.md)

## [Shushlaika AI](./Shushlaika%20AI) — upload a spreadsheet, talk to it

![Shushlaika AI](<Shushlaika AI/screenshots/workspace.png>)

A data workspace where you upload a CSV and ask an AI assistant to analyze, clean, or transform it in plain language. It picks its own method per request, reads your actual data before answering, and never overwrites — every change becomes a new version.

→ [Read more](<./Shushlaika AI/README.md>)

## [Ground Truth](./Ground%20Truth) — find engineering problems before they become obvious

![Ground Truth](<Ground Truth/screenshots/landing.png>)

Searches thousands of GitHub issues, Stack Overflow questions, and technical discussions for recurring engineering problems — and backs every conclusion with real, linked evidence instead of a model's guess. Cross-source confirmation and trend tracking separate real patterns from noise.

→ [Read more](<./Ground Truth/README.md>)
